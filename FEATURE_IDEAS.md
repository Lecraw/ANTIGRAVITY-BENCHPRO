# BenchPro Feature Ideas

Backlog of feature ideas for future development.

---

## 4. Skill Trees (Like a Video Game)

Players upgrade their skills like a game.

**Categories:**
- Shooting
- Defense
- Playmaking
- Athleticism
- Basketball IQ

**Example progression:**
- Level 3 Shooting unlocks: Catch & Shoot badge, Range Boost
- Level 5 Defense unlocks: Lockdown badge

Makes development visual and rewarding.

---

## 5. Streak System

Simple but addictive. Track streaks like:

- 🔥 Practice streak
- 🔥 Film study streak
- 🔥 Workout streak

**Example:**
```
Workout Streak
Day 6 🔥
+30 XP bonus tomorrow
```

Players don't want to lose streaks. Apps like Duolingo use this because it works.

---

## 6. Hustle Leaderboard

Most players only see points scored. This app could highlight effort stats:

- Deflections
- Charges
- Rebounds
- Loose balls
- Screen assists

**Example leaderboard:**
```
Hustle Leaderboard
1. Jayden – 34
2. Leo – 31
3. Marcus – 29
```

Motivates defense and effort, not just scoring.
